The brand story

Move from a generic wellness template to a clear, credible product story.

The original screenshots combine fitness, wellness and nutrition into one broad message. This version keeps the warm editorial feel while giving the nutraceutical product a central role.

Our approach

Useful information, presented with warmth.

Every section has a specific job: introduce the product, explain the formulation, reduce uncertainty, show practical use and make the final action obvious. This structure is easier to maintain as the product catalogue grows.

Design principles

Four principles guide the build.

01

Clarity

Visitors should understand the product and the next step without hunting through decorative sections.

02

Credibility

Use verified facts, genuine testimonials and real certificates instead of placeholder statistics or generic claims.

03

Performance

Prefer small local assets, lazy loading, explicit image dimensions and zero unnecessary framework overhead.

Built to evolve

A simple foundation for the next phase of the brand.

The same CSS system can support additional products, bundle pages, educational content, a store and campaign landing pages without introducing a framework.
