What is Bomin Fit?

Health, fitness and wellness built around three pillars.

01

Body

Physical exercise and active lifestyle support that encourages better movement, strength and daily consistency.

02

Mind

Mental exercise, awareness and healthy everyday habits designed to support clarity, routine and resilience.

03

Nutrition

Nutrition awareness and healthier everyday food habits that complement movement and lifestyle follow-through.

Bomin Fit Program

A clear, supportive programme for everyday wellness.

Bomin Fit offers both online and offline participation, helping people begin at their own pace with practical guidance in movement, mindfulness and nutrition.

01

Online programme

People can join Bomin Fit from home and follow a structured wellness journey built around body exercise, mental exercise and nutrition awareness.

02

Offline programme

Community-based support is available through contact with the Bomin Fit team and local trainers working in communities.

03

Trainer programme

People who want to become a trainer can learn the approach and explore local centre opportunities.
