Bomin Fit Program

A welcoming wellness programme for body, mind and nutrition.

Bomin Fit is a health, fitness and wellness programme designed to support balanced daily routines through movement, awareness and healthier food habits.

Three pillars

The complete Bomin Fit approach.

BODY

Physical exercise

Support for movement, strength, flexibility and an active lifestyle that fits daily life.

MIND

Mental exercise

Awareness, consistency and healthy habits that encourage a calmer, more structured routine.

NUTRITION

Nutrition awareness

Practical guidance on everyday food choices that support long-term wellness and sustainable habits.

How it works

Online and offline pathways, designed for real life.

Bomin Fit is available online and offline so people can join from home or connect with the programme in their local community.

ONLINE

Join from home

People can participate from home with a wellness-focused routine that includes body exercise, mental exercise and nutrition awareness.

OFFLINE

Community support

People can contact Bomin Fit to learn more about local programme options and trainer-led community activity.
