About

Bomin Fit is a wellness programme built to support everyday health.

Bomin Fit brings together body exercise, mental exercise and nutrition awareness into a clear and practical daily experience.

Our foundation

A practical programme for long-term wellbeing.

Bomin Fit is designed to help people build sustainable habits across movement, mindset and nutrition. The programme is presented as a balanced wellness model with simple participation pathways.

Brand positioning

Bomin Fit is the primary identity, not BB Powder alone.

BB Powder remains a supporting product within the wider wellness brand. Bomin Fit is positioned first as a health, fitness and wellness programme built around body, mind and nutrition.
